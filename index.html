3 The chapter story in four lines

🏺 Ancient thinkers

Divide matter again and again — you reach an indivisible piece: parmanu in India, atomos in Greece.

🔬 Thomson, 1897

Atoms contain light negative particles (electrons) sitting inside a ball of positive charge — the plum pudding.

🥇 Rutherford, 1911

Gold foil experiment: alpha particles bounce back from a tiny, dense, positive nucleus. Atom is mostly empty space.

🛰️ Bohr, 1913

Electrons live in fixed shells of definite energy and never fall in — atoms are stable. Neutrons follow in 1932.
